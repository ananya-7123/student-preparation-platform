Each CSV follows the project format metadata: Company, Year, Section, Topic, Difficulty, Question type, Question, Answer/solution, Source. An additional Question status column distinguishes REPORTED candidate-experience questions from PRACTICE questions.

IMPORTANT DATA RULES
- REPORTED means candidate-reported, not an official company paper or guaranteed KIIT-specific PYQ.
- PRACTICE means representative preparation content, not claimed as asked by that company.
- The source document does not provide answers/solutions or difficulty for most questions; these are explicitly marked as not provided/not specified rather than invented.
- Where the source did not give a recruitment year, Year is marked Not specified.
- Some combined prompts cover multiple topics; they are assigned one primary folder for organization.
- Company source URLs are retained in the Source column where supplied in the original document.
