# TCS NQT — Company & Recruitment Information

## TCS NQT — KIIT 2027 Recruitment Drive
- Batch: 2027 graduating students
- Qualifications: B.Tech, B.E., M.Tech, M.E., MCA, M.Sc./M.S.
- Branch: All disciplines/departments/streams
- Test: TCS National Qualifier Test (TCS NQT)
- Date: 19 July 2026
- Mode: Physical / in-centre at KIIT Campus
- Registration deadline: 15 May 2026, 2:00 PM
- Portal: TCS NextStep, IT category
- Candidates must click “Apply for Drive”.

## Eligibility
- Minimum 60% or 6 CGPA throughout academics.
- Maximum 1 pending/active backlog at selection; no pending backlog at joining.
- No extended education.
- Academic gaps must be declared; breaks must not exceed 24 months.
- Full-time courses only.
- NIOS candidates are eligible if other courses are full-time.
- Up to 2 years prior work experience for postgraduate students.
- Age: 18–28 years.

## TCS NQT Test Pattern
### Foundation — 75 minutes
- Numerical Ability — 25 min
- Verbal Ability — 25 min
- Reasoning Ability — 25 min

### Advanced — 115 minutes
- Advanced Quantitative & Reasoning Ability — 25 min
- Advanced Coding — 90 min

**Total: 190 minutes**

Official TCS information states that Advanced is mandatory for candidates aspiring to Digital or Prime.

## Hiring Categories
**Prime:** complex projects, advanced technologies/algorithms, emerging technology, system design, automation and security.

**Digital:** solution development/integration, modern technology initiatives, product development and technology frameworks/tools.

**Ninja:** application development/maintenance, systems engineering and next-generation technology exposure.

## Application & Selection
1. Register/login on TCS NextStep.
2. Register under IT.
3. Generate/retain the TCS Reference ID.
4. Click “Apply for Drive”.
5. Complete required application details and preferences.
6. Track application status.

Official TCS information states that candidates qualify for Prime/Digital/Ninja interviews based on test performance. The official FAQ states that the standard Integrated Test Pattern applies regardless of selected skill; shortlisted candidates are evaluated predominantly on the selected skill during interview.

## Documents
Keep original Class X, XII, Diploma (if applicable), UG, PG (if applicable) marksheets and degree certificates ready. Work-experience candidates should also keep relevant experience/relieving documents.

## Important Notes
- Test-related communication is provided through TCS iON.
- TCS states that it does not ask candidates to deposit money for employment offers.
- TCS states that it is not associated with external agencies for conducting interviews or making employment offers.
- Eligibility is checked at multiple stages.

## Scope
KIIT notice is the primary source for this specific 2027 drive. Official TCS sources supplement general/current NQT process information only.
