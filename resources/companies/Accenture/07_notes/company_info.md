## Eligibility Criteria

- Only full-time education is eligible.
- Part-time education is not eligible.
- Distance education is not eligible.
- Diploma alone is not eligible.
- Lateral-entry candidates are eligible if they completed a full-time Diploma followed by a B.E./B.Tech degree through an approved full-time Diploma-to-Degree pathway.
- Candidate should not have more than 23 months of full-time experience.
- No active backlogs during application or onboarding.
- Candidate should not have appeared for the Accenture recruitment assessment/interview process in the previous three months.
- Candidate must complete the relevant degree within the stipulated duration.
- There should be no gaps during the degree.
- Indian citizenship is required.
- Bhutan and Nepal nationals can work in India without obtaining a work visa; other foreign nationals require a work visa, OCI, or PIO card according to the notification.
- Candidates must provide accurate and complete information through Superset. Incorrect or misleading information may result in disqualification.

## Selection Process

## Documents / Verification

A PAN card with the candidate's photograph is mandatory for registration and document verification.

- E-PAN is accepted.
- PAN application acknowledgement is not accepted.
- The notification states that a PAN card made when the student was a minor will not be accepted.
- PAN card with photograph is stated to be the only identity proof accepted during offer and document verification.

## General Accenture Assessment Information

Accenture's general recruitment information states that technical assessments may evaluate:
- Problem-solving
- Logical reasoning
- Applied technical knowledge
- Approach to solving problems in a coding environment

Accenture states that assessments are generally completed online in one sitting and that most assessments take approximately 30–90 minutes, depending on the assessment/proficiency level. These general details should not automatically be treated as the exact timing or pattern of the KIIT recruitment assessment.
